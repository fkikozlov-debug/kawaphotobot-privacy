Privacy Policy – KawaiiPhotoBot 🎀

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Introduction

KawaiiPhotoBot is a Telegram bot that can transform text into cute aesthetic styles, edit photos and generate AI images. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the bot.

2. Information That May Be Processed

  • Your Telegram user ID.
  • Messages and commands you send to the bot.
  • Photos and images you upload for editing.
  • Prompts and descriptions you submit for image generation.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to respond to commands, transform text, edit or generate images, and operate and maintain the bot.

4. Photos and AI Services

Uploaded images and prompts may be sent to third-party AI providers when needed to perform the requested editing or generation. Their processing is also subject to their own privacy terms. Do not upload images or information you are not comfortable sharing with the bot and its service providers.

5. Sharing and Sale

The bot operator does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers only as needed to provide the bot's features or comply with legal obligations.

6. Storage and Security

How long information is retained depends on the bot's actual hosting, logging and AI-service configuration. Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information. Do not assume uploaded content is immediately deleted unless the bot operator has configured and verified deletion.

7. Children's Privacy

Do not send sensitive personal information, private documents or images that you do not have permission to share.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. The latest version should be published at this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the KawaiiPhotoBot administrator through the bot's Telegram profile.
